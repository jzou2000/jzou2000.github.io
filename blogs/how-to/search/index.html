Search In Google and Obsidian

AND vs OR conditions

Goal Syntax in Gemini / Google Syntax in Obsidian
Both words anywhere "A" "B" A B
Both in same paragraph (Not supported) block:(A B)
Both on same line (Not supported) line:(A B)
Exact phrase match "A B" "A B"

Note:

  • Sometimes “A” and “B” are in far distance in an article, that makes you think only “A” or “B” is hit
  • If AND condition fails, OR is tried.
