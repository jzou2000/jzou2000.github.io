Waypipe

Wayland does not support native network transparency the way X11 did.

Use waypipe to forward or redirect applications to another host.

Waypipe acts as a transparent proxy. It intercepts the Wayland protocol messages and pixel buffers, compresses them (using video encoders like H.264 or LZ4), and pipes them securely over an SSH connection.

waypipe ssh user@remote-host gedit

The remote application opens on your local Wayland desktop as an independent, floating window.

Note:
waypipe needs to be installed on both client and server.
