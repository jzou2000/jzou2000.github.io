In your original prompt, a few key issues confuse the model:

  1. Contradictory Angles: Asking for the camera to be “behind the net” while zooming back from a front close-up forces the AI to place the net between the camera and the man. In the generated video, the man is actually standing in front of the net relative to the lens, ruining the court depth.

  2. Action Overload: Combining a face zoom, camera pullback, breathing delay, ball toss, overhead swing, contact, and follow-through across exact timestamps overloads the spatio-temporal attention mechanism. The model morphs his swing into a side-arm forehand/overhead hybrid without ever rendering the toss.

Core Fixes for LTX Prompts

  • Simplify Visual Spatial Anchors: State the persistent camera position relative to the environment first, rather than dynamic switches.

  • Isolate Action Stages using Structural Anchoring: Use step-by-step sequential descriptors (e.g., [0-1s], [1-2s]) or clear physical cues without competing temporal mandates.

  • Explicit Mechanics: Explicitly state the tennis ball is tossed into the air before the racquet moves overhead.
