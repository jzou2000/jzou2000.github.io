Ex - Basic Text to Video (2 Stages)

In fact, 2 stages workflow is LTX standard or recommended workfow

  1. Generate video at half resolution
  2. Upscale video by 2 as the second stage

The benefits:

  • use lower VRAM
  • speedup the whole generating
