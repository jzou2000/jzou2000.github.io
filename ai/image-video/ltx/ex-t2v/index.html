Ex - Basic Text to Video

This is the settings and prompt used for text-to-video example in the above tutorial.

size: 1280x736
length: 120  5x24
fps: 24

prompt: 花园里有一个年轻的亚裔女性，镜头推进，特写女人脸部靠近一朵红色玫瑰，女人说：这花好香啊。
  • width/height must be 32x
  • fps=24
  • frames = fps * seconds + 1 (total frames must be 8x +1)
  • distilled model
    • cfg=1
    • steps=8
